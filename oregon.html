Oregon 200 — Verification Pass Summary (2026-08-30)

Final tally: 140 Verified / 60 Downgraded across the 200. Oregon's biggest names (Nike, Precision Castparts, Daimler/Freightliner, Lithia, Greenbrier, Schnitzer/Radius, Tillamook co-op, Harry & David) were pre-parked in Tier C; the A/B leads verified at a high rate, led by forest-products families, wineries/breweries, and specialty food.

Downgrades beyond the pre-screened Tier C — out-of-state parent / public / PE / foreign / acquired:

  • Public: Columbia Sportswear, Willamette Valley Vineyards.
  • PE / acquired: Les Schwab (Meritage), Oregon Tool / Blount (American Securities), Vigor Industrial (Carlyle/Titan lineage), Alpenrose Dairy (acquired), Howard S. Wright (Balfour Beatty), Ater Wynne (merged into Buchalter).
  • Foreign parent: New Seasons Market (Emart/S. Korea), Da Yang Seafood (China), Danner (ABC-Mart/Japan), Rodda Paint (Cloverdale/Canada), Warn Industries (Dover, public).
  • Out-of-state HQ / parent: Sierra Pacific (CA), Stoneway Electric (WA), CD Baby / Downtown (NY), Sunriver Resort (out-of-state owner).

Strongest verified clusters to lead the Oregon plan: family forest-products / sawmills (Roseburg, Stimson, Hampton, Collins, Swanson, Freres, Seneca, Murphy); wine + craft beverage (King Estate, Sokol Blosser, Adelsheim, Deschutes, Rogue, pFriem); specialty food (Bob's Red Mill ESOP, Oregon Fruit, Truitt Brothers, Rogue/Face Rock creameries); and a deep bench of family GCs and mechanical/electrical subs. Carry-forward caution: A-dec, Leatherman, Benchmade and the larger sawmills are family-owned but several sit near or above band — confirm the in-band operating unit before a call.

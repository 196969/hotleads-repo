Taxonomy additions from this market (carry forward to remaining nine states):

  • Second-order missed acquisition (new, highest-priority addition) — a family-acquirer disposition is not a resolution, it's a new row to check. Kar's Nuts looked like a clean same-state strategic buyer of Sanders Candy in 2018, then was itself swept into UK private equity (CapVest) in 2022. Every Tier B "family bought out founding family" case now needs the ACQUIRER's own cap table verified, not just the target's.
  • Recency-weighted search is mandatory, not optional for any large, old, family-named row — five catches in this market alone: Alro Steel (Aug 2026 PE majority stake, weeks before this file was built), Android Industries (Dec 2025 merger into Voltava), Standard Electric + Madison Electric (Apr 2024, same Sonepar transaction, two century-old 4th-gen family names gone with zero surface signal), Crystal Flash (2024 ESOP conversion).
  • Retained name, replaced family — confirmed three times in one market (Toebe Construction, Gardner-White, Preston Feather). Common enough in old Michigan firms specifically to check by default whenever a company's marketing leans on founder-era heritage language.
  • Founder-to-professional succession stays Tier A — target the incoming operator, not a family narrative that no longer exists (Rockford, Erhardt, Wolverine, Anlaan). A recently-installed professional with something to prove is arguably a better door than an inherited one.
  • Grower cooperative → Tier B, not C — co-op board fiduciary framing is closer to the ESOP pitch than a dead end; member-owners feel cost pressure directly (Graceland Fruit, Shoreline Fruit, Cherry Central, Michigan Sugar).
  • Minority foreign investment vs. full acquisition — check the size of the stake, not just its presence, before cutting to C (Short's Brewing: 19.9% to Heineken-linked Lagunitas, founder retains ~80% control).

Open action for the Second Wave list: run the acquirer's-own-cap-table check against every Tier B family-acquirer case — Adkev, Sunkey, Baird & Warner, Perricone, Heidelberg, the Shouneyia brothers (Holiday Market, #133, already carries this exact caution in-file).

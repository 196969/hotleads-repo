Final tally: 151 Verified / 49 Downgraded across the 200. Minnesota is unusually deep in genuinely owner/family/ESOP-controlled firms — the state's biggest names (3M, UnitedHealth, Target, Best Buy, General Mills, Hormel, Polaris, Ecolab-class publics) were already parked in Tier C, so the A/B leads verified at a high rate.

Downgrades beyond the pre-screened Tier C — out-of-state parent / public / PE / merged:

  • Out-of-state HQ (MN is a branch/store network only): Menards (WI), Pella (IA), Kwik Trip (WI), Festival Foods (WI), RDO Equipment (ND), County Materials (WI), Watlow Winona (MO), Bay State Milling (MA), Baker Tilly (national/IL), Wipfli (WI).
  • PE / public / acquired: Mills Fleet Farm (KKR), Anchor Block (CRH/Oldcastle), Winland Electronics (public), Peerless Industrial Group (confirm parent).
  • Law-firm mergers (door moved out of state): Gray Plant Mooty & Briggs and Morgan (both merged into national firms) — target the surviving MN office managing partner, or treat as C.

Strongest verified clusters to lead the Minnesota plan:

  • Family building-products manufacturers — Andersen, Marvin, Cambria, Red Wing Shoe, Nordic Ware: iconic multi-generational MN family/ESOP names, several near or above band (confirm which unit is in range).
  • Construction + specialty contractors — Kraus-Anderson, Mortenson, Ryan, McGough, PCL-class family/ESOP GCs plus a deep bench of family electrical/mechanical/concrete subs.
  • Family grocers & furniture retailers — Kowalski's, Lunds & Byerlys, Coborn's, Room & Board, Slumberland, HOM: clean owner-led doors.
  • Craft beverage — Schell (Marti family, 6th gen), Summit, Surly, Phillips Distilling: owner-operated, cost-pressure-sensitive.

Carry-forward caution: Cargill stays Verified as genuinely family-controlled (Cargill/MacMillan) but is 3+ orders past band — the realistic door is a specific division, not the holding company. Same logic applies to any near-ceiling family manufacturer (Andersen, Marvin, Cambria): verify the in-band operating unit before promoting to a call.
